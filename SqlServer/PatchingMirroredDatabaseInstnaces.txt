Patching mirrored database instnaces
-----------------------------------------

1.  Remove witness
2.  Safety level full
3.  Wait for synchronis mode
4.  Pause mirroring session
5.  Backup
6.  Patch mirror
7.  Resume mirroring session
8.  Manual failover
9.  DBCC checkdb
10. Pause mirroring session
11. Patch
12. Resume mirroring session
13. Change safety level (if done in step 2)
14. Add witness
